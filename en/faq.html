Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before playing the Ubicá el Sol precision challenge.

What is Ubicá el Sol?

It is an original precision challenge. You place the sun in the centre of the Argentine flag and adjust its size to get as close as possible to the ideal proportion. At the end of the rounds you get a precision percentage.

How do you play?

Drag the sun with the mouse or your finger to move it. Adjust the size with the slider and tap "Confirm result" to see your score. With a keyboard you can move the sun using the arrow keys.

How much can I play?

As many attempts as you want. Besides classic mode there is a quick mode, a time attack and a daily challenge where everyone plays the same flag.

How is the score calculated?

The score combines how close the sun centre was to the ideal centre and how similar its size was. When both apply, position weighs 70% and size 30%. The final result is the average of the rounds.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. The game is free and your best score is stored locally on your own device.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The challenge is designed for touch screens and works in modern mobile, tablet and desktop browsers.

Is this an official site?

No. Ubicá el Sol is an independent, original project. It has no official relationship with other games, creators or brands mentioned in the informational pages.

Still curious? You can play anyway

Attempts are free and there is no sign-up.
