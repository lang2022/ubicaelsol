Centra el Sol: what the viral challenge is about

In October 2026 the name "Centra el Sol" grew quickly in Argentine searches. Here is what it is, and how to try a similar precision challenge without depending on third parties.

Independence notice: this site is an original, unofficial project. It does not belong to, represent or endorse the original "Centra el Sol" game, its creators or any associated brand.

What is Centra el Sol?

It is a short precision game: an Argentine flag appears, and you must place the sun in the correct spot with the correct size. When you confirm, the game returns a percentage showing how close you got. Its simplicity and its easy-to-share result are why it spread quickly on social media.

Why did it go viral?

Several factors combine in challenges like this one:

  • It is understood in seconds and needs no long explanation.
  • The result is a number, and numbers invite comparison.
  • The goal — placing the sun in the centre of the flag — is easy to share as a challenge among friends.
  • The theme is culturally close to an Argentine audience.

This type of dynamic usually produces sharp, short-lived search spikes.

Where can I play the original?

The original game that caused the search spike does not belong to this site. If you are looking for the original game, the right thing to do is go directly to its creator. This site neither offers nor replicates that game.

What this site offers

Ubicá el Sol is an independent take on the same general idea of precision, but with its own development, design and rules:

  • A three-round format instead of a single attempt.
  • Quick mode, time attack and a daily challenge.
  • A score that combines position and size.
  • An interface adapted to mobile devices.

If you liked the idea of placing the sun on the flag, you can try this site's original challenge or first learn how to play.

About the Sun of May

The Argentine flag features the Sun of May, an emblem with rays representing the sun of the May Revolution. Knowing its shape and proportion helps you score better in precision challenges like this one.
