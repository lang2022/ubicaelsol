Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy explains in plain language what data is used when you play Ubicá el Sol.

Summary

The game runs directly in your browser. We do not require registration, we do not ask for your name or contact details, and we do not sell personal information.

Data stored on your device

We store a minimal amount of information in your browser local storage so the game works better:

  • Your best score.
  • The number of games played.
  • Your language preference.
  • Your advertising consent choice.

This data stays on your device and you can remove it at any time by clearing your browser data.

Analytics

With your consent we may collect aggregated, anonymous usage data, such as how often a page is opened or whether a game is completed. This helps improve the game and is not used to identify you personally.

Advertising

With your consent the site may show ads through third-party providers. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads and measure their performance. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time by clearing site data, or manage preferences in your browser settings.

Consent

Advertising and optional analytics only load after you accept. If you choose "Essential only", the game still works fully using local storage only.

Third-party services

If analytics or advertising tools are integrated, they may collect information under their own policies. This page will be updated to reflect any change.

Children

We do not request or store personal information from minors. If a responsible adult notices that information was submitted by mistake, they can contact us to have it removed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect technical or legal changes. The update date will appear at the top.

Contact

For privacy questions, write to us from the contact page.
