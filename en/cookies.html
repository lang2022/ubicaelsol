Cookie Policy

This page explains what cookies are and how they may be used on Ubicá el Sol.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. They are used, for example, to remember preferences or measure site usage. We also use local storage for similar purposes.

What we use

  • Essential: let the game remember your best score, games played, chosen language and your consent choice.
  • Analytics: if enabled, help understand in aggregate how the site is used.
  • Advertising: if ads are shown, providers may use cookies to display and measure ads.

How to manage them

You can delete or block cookies from your browser settings. If you block all cookies, the game still works, but it will not remember your scores or preferences.

Consent

Non-essential cookies and advertising scripts only load after you accept the consent notice. You can accept or decline, and change your decision later by clearing site data or using the browser settings.

More information

For details about data handling, see our privacy policy.
