Frequently asked questions

What is Ubicá el Sol?

It is an original precision challenge. You place the sun in the centre of the Argentine flag and adjust its size to get as close as possible to the ideal proportion.

How do you play?

Drag the sun with the mouse or your finger, adjust the size with the slider and tap "Confirm result" to see your score.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. The game is free and your best score is stored locally on your own device.

How is the score calculated?

The score combines how close the sun centre was to the ideal centre and how similar its size was to the ideal proportion.

Is this an official site?

No. Ubicá el Sol is an independent, original project. It has no official relationship with other games, creators or brands.
