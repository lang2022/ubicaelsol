How to play Ubicá el Sol

The challenge is easy to understand and hard to master: get the sun close to the centre of the flag with the right proportion. This guide explains each step and how the score is calculated.

Goal of the game

Place the sun symbol in the centre of the Argentine flag and adjust its size so it matches the ideal proportion as closely as possible. At the end of three rounds you get a precision percentage that combines both factors.

The flag never changes, the view does. The answer is always the true centre of the flag and the true size of the Sun of May. What changes each round is how the flag is presented: it appears at a different zoom and position, so the centre of the board is not the centre of the flag. Do not aim for the middle of the screen — aim for the middle of the flag.

The flag follows the official 5:8 proportion and the Sun of May measures 5/6 of the white stripe height, which is 5/18 of the total flag height.

Steps to play

  1. Pick a mode. Quick (one round), Classic (no pressure), Time attack (20 seconds per round) or Daily challenge (everyone plays the same flag that day).
  2. Move the sun. Drag it with the mouse or your finger. With a keyboard you can select it and move it with the arrow keys.
  3. Adjust the size. Use the slider. In the first round of a full challenge the size is already set, so you only focus on position.
  4. Confirm the result. You will see your score and can share it or try again.

How the score is calculated

Each round is evaluated with two criteria:

  • Position: how close the sun centre was to the ideal centre of the flag.
  • Size: how similar the sun diameter was to the ideal proportion.

When both criteria apply, the score combines 70% position and 30% size. In the first round of the full challenge the size is given, so only position is scored.

The final result is the average of the rounds.

Tip: find the exact centre of the flag first, then adjust the size. Moving both at the same time usually hurts precision.

Tips to improve

  • Turn on the guides if you need a visual reference for the centre line.
  • On touch screens, press gently so you do not drag too far.
  • In time attack, decide the position before the clock runs down.
  • Repeat the daily challenge to compare against your own previous results.

How long is a game?

A full three-round game takes less than a minute in classic mode. A quick challenge takes about 20 seconds.
